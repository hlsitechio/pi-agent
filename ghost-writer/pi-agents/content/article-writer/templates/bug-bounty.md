# Article Structure

## The Setup
What program, what drew you to it. (Sanitize — no real target names unless public writeup)

## Recon Phase
Tools used, methodology. Show actual commands (redacted targets).

## The Discovery
The moment you found it. What tipped you off. The "aha" moment.

## Exploitation / PoC
Step by step. Screenshots/command output. Make it reproducible (on a test target).

## The Report
How you wrote it. What made the triager accept it fast.

## Lessons & Tips
What you'd do differently. What others can learn.
