# Article Structure

## Hook
A specific moment, release, or discovery that sparked this article.

## What Changed
The new development — model, tool, technique, paradigm shift.

## Why It Matters
Connect to the reader's world. How does this affect developers, security pros, businesses?

## Hands-On Section
Show, don't tell:
- Installation/setup steps
- Code snippets
- Benchmark comparisons
- Real output examples

## Outlook
Where this is heading. Your take. Bold predictions welcome.
