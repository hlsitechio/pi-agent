# Article Structure

## The Threat
What are we protecting against? Make it relatable. Use recent news.

## Reality Check
How bad is it really? Data, statistics, real incidents. No fear-mongering without evidence.

## Practical Steps (main body)
Numbered steps anyone can follow:
1. Easy wins first (settings changes, defaults)
2. Medium effort (tool installation, habit changes)
3. Advanced (for the truly motivated)

## Recommended Tools
Specific tools with pros/cons. Be honest about trade-offs.

## Summary
"If you do nothing else, do these 3 things."
